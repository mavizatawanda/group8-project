How Cryptographic Verification Works

A decentralized, automated verification flow from university registrar to employer verification.

Step 1
🏛️

Institutional Issuance

The accredited registrar registers the graduate's award. A deterministic SHA-256 cryptographic signature is computed over student identity and academic parameters.

Step 2
⛓️

Blockchain Mining

The qualification fingerprint is mined into an immutable block containing the previous block hash and proof-of-integrity nonce, preventing any future record alterations.

Step 3
🔍

Authorized Verification

Accredited verifiers, employers and institutions sign in to verify certificate serial numbers, inspect SHA-256 digital hashes, and evaluate real-time AI fraud risk scores.
